The Medical category provides rigorous, source-driven coverage intended for clinicians, researchers, and readers seeking technical depth. We report on clinical trials, regulatory decisions, diagnostic and therapeutic innovation, and studies of health systems and delivery, emphasising methodological transparency and patient-centred implications.

When covering clinical research we link to trial registrations, full-text publications, and regulatory summaries where available. We describe endpoints, sample sizes, and statistical methods so readers can assess the reliability and applicability of findings. For regulatory decisions we cite the agency notice and include the text of any conditions or post-market requirements.

Our medical reporting also covers implementation challenges — how a new therapy or diagnostic is adopted in practice, cost and access considerations, and workforce impacts. We avoid sensational reporting and provide clear signposts for clinicians seeking more technical resources, including links to guidelines and professional societies.

Medical professionals who wish to contribute expert analysis or suggest topics should contact `editorial@wesearchai.com` with their credentials and supporting documentation. We require transparent disclosures of any conflicts of interest or funding related to the subject matter.

Our medical features sometimes include appendices or resource lists for clinicians, with links to guideline documents, registry entries, and methodological notes. This is intended to support further reading and to allow practitioners to assess the applicability of research findings to their own settings. When discussing trials or device approvals we include links to manufacturer documentation and regulatory summaries where available.

Editorial process and peer review

Medical pieces on WeSearchAI follow a stricter editorial pathway: technical features undergo targeted peer review by practising clinicians or researchers when they contain clinical recommendations or technical protocols. We require contributors to disclose any clinical affiliations or funding, and we archive reviewer notes alongside long-form work where permissions allow. Clinicians who wish to propose peer-review contributions or submit technical commentaries should contact `editorial@wesearchai.com` with credentials and supporting materials.

Resource and guideline links

For regulatory and clinical references we link to primary regulatory documents (FDA, EMA, PMDA), clinical guidelines, and trial registries to enable direct verification. Where device approvals are discussed we include regulatory summary texts and post-market safety notices when available.
