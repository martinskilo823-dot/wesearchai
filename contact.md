# Contact WeSearchAI

WeSearchAI welcomes messages from readers, advertisers, contributors, and researchers. Use the contact channels below so we can direct your request to the right team and respond promptly.

## Primary contacts

- **General enquiries & partnerships:** info@wesearchai.com
- **Advertising & sponsorship:** advertising@wesearchai.com
- **Editorial / corrections / submissions:** editorial@wesearchai.com
- **Technical & bug reports:** tech@wesearchai.com
- **Legal, DMCA & copyright:** legal@wesearchai.com
- **Phone (optional):** +2348132329272
## Editorial team and office hours

Our editorial team includes an editor-in-chief, managing editor, and a small team of reporters and editors who handle submissions and corrections. We operate Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 local time for enquiries submitted via email; complex legal or partnership requests may require additional time for review.

### Escalations and urgent requests

If you have an urgent legal or safety concern (for example: immediate copyright, privacy breach, or content posing imminent risk of harm), include "URGENT" in the subject line and mark the message clearly; our legal team will prioritise review and respond as quickly as possible.
When emailing, include a clear subject line and the following for faster service: your name, a concise description of the request, relevant URLs, and a contact email or phone number.

## How to submit editorial corrections or fact-check requests

If you find an error in an article, send the article URL, a brief description of the issue, and source references to `editorial@wesearchai.com`. We aim to acknowledge corrections within 3 business days and publish any verified corrections as appropriate.

## Advertising enquiries

For advertising rates, placement options, and sponsorship opportunities, contact `advertising@wesearchai.com`. Include campaign timelines, target audience, and any creative specifications.

## Legal / DMCA notices

If you believe material on this site infringes your copyright, send a DMCA notice to `legal@wesearchai.com` with "DMCA Notice" in the subject and include the required elements under the DMCA (identification of the copyrighted work, location of the infringing material, contact information, and a good-faith statement). For guidance, see: https://support.google.com/legal/answer/1120734

## Response times

We aim to respond to general and editorial enquiries within 2–3 business days. Legal requests and complex partnership proposals may take longer.

## Privacy

Personal data submitted through these channels is processed in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). If you are in the EU/EEA or California you have rights described in that policy (access, correction, deletion, etc.).

## Press and media enquiries

Members of the press should email `editorial@wesearchai.com` with "Press Inquiry" in the subject line and include the outlet name, reporter contact details, and a short description of the request. If you need images, logos, or a media kit, indicate that in the message.

## Submitting story ideas or guest contributions

We welcome well-researched pitches from independent journalists and subject-matter experts. To submit a pitch, email `editorial@wesearchai.com` and include:

- A one-paragraph pitch describing the story angle
- Links to two recent writing samples or portfolio
- Suggested length and any relevant deadlines

Accepted guest contributions must follow our editorial standards and provide verifiable sources. We do not accept paid guest posts disguised as editorial content; sponsored content should be discussed with `advertising@wesearchai.com`.

## Accessibility and accommodations

If you require content in an alternative format or need accessibility accommodations to use our site, email `tech@wesearchai.com` with details and we will make reasonable efforts to assist.

## DMCA / Copyright takedown procedure (more detail)

If you are submitting a DMCA notice, include all of the following in your email to `legal@wesearchai.com`:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
2. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate the material (URLs preferred).
3. Your contact information (name, address, telephone number, email).
4. A statement that you have a good‑faith belief that use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on behalf of the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We will review notices promptly and take action in accordance with applicable law.
